I am a Postdoctoral Scholar in the Department of Civil and Environmental Engineering at Stanford University and a [Stanford Impact Labs](https://impact.stanford.edu/) Postdoctoral Fellow. I am part of the [Stanford Sustainable Systems Lab](https://s3l.stanford.edu/).

Previously, I received my Ph.D. in Civil and Environmental Engineering from Stanford University in 2025. I obtained my M.Sc. and B.Sc. in Electrical Engineering from Shanghai Jiao Tong University in 2018 and 2015, respectively.

My research examines the nexus between climate change, energy systems, and human adaptation through interdisciplinary methods spanning artificial intelligence, engineering, and social sciences. I focus on three interconnected areas:

- **Projecting climate risks to infrastructure**: Developing publicly available datasets of infrastructure-critical weather extremes for direct integration into climate risk assessments by researchers and industry practitioners.
- **Quantifying climate impacts on energy systems**: Applying data-driven methods to large-scale power infrastructure and end-user consumption data to understand how climate change affects energy reliability and affordability.
- **Enabling community adaptation**: Combining engineering-based technology assessments with social science survey methods to understand how end users and communities can leverage distributed energy resources, including solar, battery storage, and electric vehicles, for resilience and cost savings.
